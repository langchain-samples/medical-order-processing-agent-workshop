# Status Update Skill

This one goes outward — to the account or the sales rep, not the internal desk. Shorter and plainer than an exception note.

## Format

- **Subject line**: order number plus current state in a handful of words
- **Where it stands**: two or three sentences in plain language, no internal jargon
- **What we need from you**: a short list, or "nothing at this time"
- **Expected next update**: when they will hear from us again

## Tone

- Plain language — no HCPCS codes, payer policy numbers, or internal system names
- Own the delay without speculating about blame or cause
- No commitments on dates, coverage, or reimbursement outcomes

## Length

- Under 120 words
- No Sources section — citations belong in the internal exception note
